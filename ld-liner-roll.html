APPLICATIONS

Where LD Liner Rolls Are Used

Delivering high-performance containment across India's leading industrial sectors

Chemical & Petrochemicals

Lining 200-liter HDPE and steel drums to contain aggressive chemicals, solvent powders, pigments, and synthetic resins without leaks.

Food Grains, Spices & Sugar

100% virgin food-contact compliant continuous barrier for large storage silos, corrugated boxes, and export gunny bag internal lining.

Carton Box & Pallet Covers

Continuous protective sleeves that drop over assembled cartons, preventing dust buildup in warehouses and rain damage on shipping docks.

Construction & Civil Engineering

Heavy-micron single-wound sheet rolls used as concrete under-slab vapor barriers, foundation moisture retarders, and plaster curing wraps.

Automotive & Machinery Parts

High-gauge anti-corrosion barrier wrapping for crankshafts, metal stamping dies, precision bearings, and export spare components.

Export Shipping & Logistics

Heavy-duty ocean freight sea container lining, preventing moisture condensation ('container rain') from ruining high-value bulk commodities.

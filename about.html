The Agola Polymer Journey

Born in the Industrial Capital of Morbi, Built for Global Standards

We started with one clear purpose: solve the rampant packaging failures in heavy manufacturing, chemicals, and agricultural exports.

Located in Morbi, Gujarat — the epicenter of India’s ceramic and export manufacturing — Agola Polymer was established to bridge the gap between low-quality compromised polythene bags and premium, high-integrity barrier packaging.

Over the years, we invested heavily in advanced multi-layer blown film extrusion machinery, skilled polymer engineers, and high-precision sealing systems. Today, we cater to hundreds of industrial clients across India, supplying robust LLDPE liners, specialized heat-shrink rolls, agricultural films, and commercial packaging bags.

Our 4-Stage Quality Extrusion Process

How raw virgin polymer granules are transformed into heavy-duty barrier packaging solutions.

01

Resin Selection & Blending

100% prime virgin LLDPE/LDPE granules are dosed with UV stabilizers, slip additives, or antistatic agents based on client specifications.

02

Precision Co-Extrusion

Molten polymer is extruded through spiral dies with automated internal bubble cooling, maintaining exact gauge consistency across the roll.

03

Corona Treatment & Sealing

Continuous surface treatment for print adhesion, followed by heavy-duty bottom seal or star seal ultrasonic welding to prevent leaks.

04

QA Lab Testing & Dispatch

Dart drop impact, elongation test, and gauge micrometry checks before rolls are moisture-wrapped, palletized, and dispatched pan-India.

Why High-Tensile LD Shrink & Stretch Films Reduce Transit Damage by 40%

Agola Polymer Logistics Team September 10, 2026 4 min read ✓ ASTM D5458 Certified

💡 Executive Summary & Key Takeaways

  • Elastic Memory Retention Beats Static Strapping: High-performance cast and blown stretch films continuously contract around settling cargo, eliminating slack that causes pallet collapse.
  • 300% Pre-Stretch Cuts Material Costs: Machine-grade LLDPE film converts 1 kg of resin into up to 4x the usable wrapping surface area, cutting film expenditure by 35–45%.
  • Critical Protection for Morbi Ceramic Tiles: A reinforced pallet wrap (23µ–29µ) locks tile boxes to the wooden pallet deck, preventing container shifting and internal chipping during export.
  • Shrink vs. Stretch Selection: Use stretch film for high-volume palletized freight and uniform cartons; use heavy-duty LD shrink film for irregular machinery, brick bundles, and glass bottle consolidation.

In global supply chains and domestic freight logistics across India, in-transit cargo damage costs manufacturers billions of rupees annually. In industrial manufacturing hubs like Morbi, Gujarat—where hundreds of metric tons of heavy ceramic tiles, bathroom sanitaryware, and chemical drums are loaded into export containers daily—transit vibrations and braking inertia frequently cause unanchored pallets to tilt, crush, and break.

Traditional steel and polyester strapping, while providing rigid perimeter holding, cannot prevent individual boxes from shifting or protect against rain, dust, and moisture. By transitioning to high-tensile LLDPE stretch film and LD heat shrink film, forward-thinking logistics directors are cutting cargo damage by over 40% while simultaneously reducing plastic material consumption.

1. The Physics of Pallet Unitization: Elastic Memory vs. Strapping

When pallets travel across bumpy highways or endure oceanic sea swelling, cartons naturally settle and compress. Static plastic or steel strapping stays at its initial circumference, creating loose slack that allows boxes to slide. In contrast, premium LLDPE stretch film operates on the principle of elastic memory recovery:

  • Active Continuous Clamping: The film is stretched during application and continuously pulls inward toward its original state, constantly gripping the boxes.
  • Multi-Directional Tensioning: Cross-wrapping spirals distribute centrifugal forces across the entire cube, turning hundreds of loose cartons into a single rigid monolithic block.
  • Pallet Base Locking (Roping): Anchoring the bottom 3–4 wraps directly around the wooden pallet stringer prevents the entire stack from sliding off the skid during forklift acceleration or abrupt truck braking.

2. Stretch Film vs. LD Shrink Film: Which One Does Your Plant Need?

While both films are manufactured from polyethylene polymers, their operational mechanics and ideal use cases differ significantly:

3. Case Study: Solving Ceramic Tile Breakage in Morbi Export Shipments

Ceramic tiles present one of the most challenging packaging loads in India due to high density, sheer weight (often exceeding 1,200 kg per pallet), and brittle glaze edges. A leading Morbi exporter was experiencing a 4.8% transit break rate in export shipping containers.

By switching from manual 15µ film to Agola Polymer’s 29µ Machine-Grade Power Pre-Stretch Film applied via an automated turntable with 250% pre-stretch and pallet-skid roping, the exporter achieved:

  • Transit Damage Reduced by 42%: Cracking and corner breakage dropped from 4.8% down to less than 0.7%.
  • 35% Reduction in Film Consumption: The machine stretch mechanism reduced total film weight from 1.2 kg to just 0.78 kg per pallet.
  • 100% Dust & Moisture Seal: Ceramic boxes arrived at destination ports with pristine unblemished branding.

4. Essential Best Practices for Plant Packaging Lines

  1. Always Overlap by at least 50%: Each successive spiral layer should cover 50% of the previous layer to build cumulative tensile containment.
  2. Lock the Skid: Minimum of 3 complete revolutions at the base, crossing over the top board of the wooden pallet.
  3. Double Wrap the Crown: Apply 2–3 wraps at the top to prevent upper layer drift during transit cornering.
  4. Maintain Proper Storage: Store film rolls vertically in clean, dry environments below 35°C to preserve tackiness and unrolling smoothness.

1. Details: Understanding High-Performance LD Liner Rolls

An LD Liner Roll (Low-Density Polyethylene & Linear Low-Density Polyethylene Liner Roll) is a continuous cylindrical roll of seamless tubular polymer film or center-folded sheeting designed for versatile industrial barrier protection. Unlike standard plastic films, industrial-grade LD liner rolls are engineered to withstand extreme physical elongation, puncture stress, and chemical permeability while serving as an indispensable protective barrier for bulk containers, drums, gaylord boxes, octabins, and sea-freight pallets.

At Agola Polymer, located in Morbi, Gujarat, our continuous LD liner rolls are extruded using advanced multi-layer blown film technology. By co-extruding 100% prime virgin LLDPE and LDPE resins, we combine the superior tensile elongation and dart-impact toughness of linear polymers with the optical clarity and clean sealability of traditional LDPE. This creates a flexible packaging solution that eliminates container pinholing, withstands rough transit shocks, and protects high-value cargo from moisture, oxygen, dust, and industrial contaminants.

⚡ 100% Virgin Polymer Resins

Formulated without recycled fillers or calcium carbonate degradation, guaranteeing uniform gauge thickness, zero foul odor, and maximum tear resistance.

✂️ On-Demand Cut-to-Length Savings

Continuous tubular format allows factory workers to cut bags to exact container heights, drastically cutting scrap material and optimizing bag usage.

🛡️ Micro-Pore Free Vapor Barrier

Ultra-low water vapor transmission rate (WVTR) prevents moisture caking of hygroscopic powders, corrosion of precision parts, and contamination.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Quick answers to technical, commercial, and dispatch inquiries regarding our LD Liner Rolls.

1. What are the primary operational benefits of using LD Liner Rolls over pre-cut bags?

Continuous LD liner rolls allow packaging operators to cut custom lengths on-demand, accommodating varying container depths and drum sizes without maintaining excessive pre-made bag inventory. It eliminates scrap, reduces packaging warehouse footprint by up to 35%, and cuts per-unit packaging costs.

2. What micron thickness is recommended for chemical drum and barrel lining?

For standard dry chemical powders and non-corrosive granular goods, 75 to 100 microns is standard. For aggressive chemical substances, viscous liquids, or sharp crystallines, heavy-duty 125 to 200 micron LLDPE rolls with superior dart impact strength are recommended.

3. Are Agola Polymer LD Liner Rolls certified for direct food contact?

Yes, our Food Grade LD Liner Rolls are extruded exclusively using 100% prime virgin polymers compliant with US FDA 21 CFR 177.1520 and Indian Standard IS 10146, ensuring zero chemical leaching, zero odor transfer, and complete food safety.

4. What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom-width LD Liner Rolls?

We provide factory-direct wholesale pricing with flexible MOQs starting from 300 kg for custom dimensions, and up to multi-ton container loads for large enterprise buyers and international exporters.

5. Can you manufacture tinted, colored, or UV-stabilized LD Liner Rolls?

Yes. While natural crystal clear is most popular for product visibility, we also manufacture milky white, opaque black, UV-inhibited, and anti-static blue liner rolls for specialized industrial and outdoor storage requirements.

6. How quickly can orders be delivered across India?

From our advanced manufacturing plant in Morbi, Gujarat, standard roll specifications are dispatched within 24 to 48 hours. Custom engineered extrusion orders are completed and dispatched within 3 to 5 business days with direct pan-India transit.

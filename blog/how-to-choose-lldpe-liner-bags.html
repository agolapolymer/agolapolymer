How to Choose the Right LLDPE Liner Bags for Food & Chemical Storage

Agola Polymer Technical Team September 15, 2026 5 min read ✓ ISO 9001:2015 Verified

💡 Executive Summary & Key Takeaways

  • Food Contact Requires 100% Virgin Resins: Always specify US FDA 21 CFR 177.1520 and IS 10146 compliance to eliminate contamination, taste alteration, and chemical migration.
  • Industrial Chemical Storage Needs High Dart Impact: Heavy-duty drum and box liners must maintain an ASTM D1709 dart impact rating above 350 grams to resist corner punctures and hydrodynamic shock during transport.
  • Optimal Micron Thickness: Light dry food ingredients (tea, dry fruits) require 40–60µ; standard chemical powders need 75–100µ; highly corrosive, liquid or viscous chemicals require 125–200µ.
  • Direct Factory Sourcing: Sourcing directly from an established extrusion plant in Morbi, Gujarat eliminates middleman markups and guarantees strict quality batch traceability.

In modern industrial and commercial operations, flexible intermediate liners represent the frontline barrier between high-value raw materials and the harsh outside environment. Whether lining 200-liter steel drums, corrugated cartons, wooden crates, or bulk Gaylord octabins, selecting the correct LLDPE (Linear Low-Density Polyethylene) liner bag directly determines product shelf life, leak prevention, and regulatory compliance.

Unfortunately, many procurement managers and plant engineers specify packaging based solely on price per kilogram without factoring in polymer virginity, tensile elongation, or dart impact resistance. This comprehensive guide walks you through the critical technical engineering parameters necessary to make the right choice.

1. Understanding the Polymer: Why LLDPE Outperforms Traditional LDPE & HDPE

LLDPE differs substantially from conventional Low-Density Polyethylene (LDPE) due to its linear polymer backbone with uniform short-chain branching. This molecular architecture gives LLDPE outstanding advantages in bulk packaging:

  • Exceptional Tensile Strength & Elongation: Up to 550% elongation at break allows the liner to stretch under heavy shifting loads without splitting.
  • Superior Puncture & Dart Impact Resistance: Drastically outperforms HDPE in resisting sharp edges, granular crystals, and mechanical handling stresses.
  • Hermetic Heat Seal Integrity: Wider sealing temperature windows ensure that bottom and gusset seals remain leak-proof even under continuous hydrostatic head pressure.

3. Micron Selection Guide for Various Packaging Containers

Choosing the correct thickness is a delicate balance between total container safety and procurement expenditure. Here are our engineering recommendations based on extensive testing at Agola Polymer:

  • Light Carton Lining (30μ to 50μ): Suitable for garments, textile fabrics, lightweight electronic parts, and dust-proofing outer packaging.
  • Standard Food Ingredient Bags (50μ to 75μ): Ideal for 25 kg bags of flour, spices, pulses, starch, and powdered dairy products requiring aroma retention and moisture barrier.
  • Standard Industrial Drum Liners (75μ to 100μ): Perfect for 50 kg to 100 kg fiber drums and mild chemical powders, preventing powder migration into drum crevices.
  • Heavy-Duty Chemical & Liquid Liners (125μ to 200μ+): Essential for 200-liter steel drums holding adhesives, lubricants, dyes, ceramic glazes, and aggressive wet compounds.

4. Sizing Calculation Formula: How to Avoid Bag Tearing & Waste

Proper measurement prevents bag rupture at carton edges and eliminates polymer waste from oversized liners:

  1. Carton/Box Width: Measure the internal box width + box depth + 50 mm (2 inches) allowance for gusset expansion.
  2. Liner Length: Measure the box height + (depth / 2) + 150 mm (6 inches) for top tie-off or heat sealing fold.
  3. Round Drum Lining: Drum Liner Width = (3.1416 × Drum Diameter) / 2 + 50 mm clearance.
